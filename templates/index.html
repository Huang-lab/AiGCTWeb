About AIGCT VEP Chat

Ask natural-language questions about how well variant effect predictors (VEPs) perform at classifying pathogenic variants for different disease areas.

Benchmark data comes from the AIGCT package. Performance is measured by ROC AUC across disease-specific variant sets.

The LLM runs locally via Ollama (model: qwen2.5:7b). No data leaves your machine.
